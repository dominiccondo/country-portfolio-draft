## Why we picked each one

**1. Apple — Tech (20%)**
The default American tech anchor: the most valuable consumer-technology company in the world, and a name every viewer already knows.

**2. Waste Management — Boring stuff (20%)**
Peak "boring but essential." Trash and recycling is the definition of an unglamorous, recession-resistant business with a wide moat — nobody stops throwing things away.

**3. Archer-Daniels-Midland — Commodity / crops (20%)**
The crops angle. The US is the world's agricultural superpower, and ADM is the giant that processes and moves the harvest — corn, soybeans, oilseeds, and food ingredients. A true American "crops" champion for this bucket.

**4. Walmart — Consumer (20%)**
The largest retailer on Earth and the backbone of American consumer spending. Defensive, dominant, and unmistakably US.

**5. JPMorgan Chase — Banking (10%)**
The biggest and best-run US bank — the blue-chip financial anchor of the portfolio.

**6. SpaceX — Wildcard / growth (10%)**
The high-octane growth bet. Space Exploration Technologies (`SPCX`) — recently public — is the world's leading private-space company (launch, Starlink). Exactly what a wildcard slot is for.
> **Note:** SpaceX was assumed private during drafting, but it trades publicly under `SPCX`, so it qualifies under the "easily tradable globally" rule.
