## Why we picked each one

**1. Infosys — Tech (20%)**
The globally-tradable face of India's massive IT-services industry. Infosys trades as a clean US ADR (`INFY` on NYSE), which is exactly why we picked it.
> **Note:** TCS (Tata Consultancy Services) is actually the *biggest* Indian IT company — but it has no US ADR and is harder to access globally, so it lost the slot to Infosys on the tradability rule.

**2. Larsen & Toubro — Boring stuff (20%)**
The iconic engineering and infrastructure bellwether that literally builds the country — ports, metros, refineries, defense. Unglamorous, essential, and a core holding in almost every serious India portfolio. Peak "boring but important."

**3. Coal India — Commodity / crops (20%)**
The world's largest coal miner, state-controlled, and a heavy dividend payer. It's an authentic commodity champion and a genuine teaching moment about how much of India's energy still runs on coal.

**4. Titan Company — Consumer / culture (20%)**
A Tata-group jewelry and watches leader that captures India's deep cultural love of gold, weddings, and status spending — the perfect "fashion/culture" pick.
> **Note:** ITC (the cigarette + FMCG conglomerate) was also considered for this slot, but its chart was uglier than Titan's, so Titan got the pick.

**5. HDFC Bank — Banking (10%)**
India's largest private-sector bank and a blue-chip name available as US ADR `HDB`. The stable, high-quality banking anchor the portfolio needs.

**6. Eternal (formerly Zomato) — Wildcard / growth (10%)**
The poster child of India's new-economy hypergrowth — food delivery plus fast-growing quick commerce (Blinkit). High-story, high-volatility, exactly what a wildcard slot is for.
> **Note:** The company renamed from Zomato to Eternal Ltd in 2025; the NSE ticker will be verified at lock-time.
