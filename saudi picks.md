## Why we picked each one

**1. Elm Company — Tech (20%)**
Saudi Arabia's tech bench is thin, which is itself a lesson — but Elm is the standout. It builds digital and IT services (much of it government-facing) and has been one of the best-performing stocks on the Tadawul. It's the most authentic way to get real Saudi "tech" exposure rather than forcing a fit.

**2. Saudi Telecom (stc) — Boring stuff (20%)**
The definition of a boring, essential blue chip. stc is the dominant telecom operator, a reliable dividend payer, and one of the most liquid names on the exchange. Telecom infrastructure is unglamorous and steady — exactly what this slot is for.

**3. Saudi Aramco — Commodity / crops (20%)**
The entire Saudi thesis in one stock. Aramco is the largest oil company on Earth, and oil *is* Saudi Arabia's commodity. There is no more authentic pick for this bucket — it's the reason the country's market exists in its current form.

**4. Almarai — Consumer (20%)**
The Middle East's largest food and dairy company, and an iconic household brand across the region. It captures Saudi consumer spending directly, and its dairy/poultry/bakery empire doubles as a "crops/food" story — a real economy stock, not just a resource play.

**5. Al Rajhi Bank — Banking (10%)**
The world's largest Islamic bank. Beyond being a blue-chip Saudi lender, it's a perfect teaching moment for the show: an introduction to Sharia-compliant finance and how banking works differently across global markets.

**6. ACWA Power — Wildcard / growth (10%)**
The Vision 2030 growth bet. ACWA develops renewable energy and water projects and is central to Saudi Arabia's push to diversify away from oil (including NEOM-linked projects). High-story, high-growth — the ideal wildcard, and a nice narrative counterweight to Aramco.

## Honest caveat (worth saying on camera)
Saudi Arabia is the least "easily tradable globally" of the three opening markets — most of these names have no US ADR, and foreign access to the Tadawul runs through the QFI framework or emerging-market index funds. The picks are still authentic and the live price data works fine via Yahoo Finance `.SR` tickers.
