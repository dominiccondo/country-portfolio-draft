## Why we picked each one

**1. Samsung Electronics — Tech (20%)**
The anchor of any Korean portfolio. The world's largest memory-chip maker and a smartphone giant, Samsung alone is worth a huge slice of the entire Korea market. If you're teaching global markets, Korea *is* Samsung. Trades as an ADR too, so it's globally recognizable.

**2. Korea Electric Power / KEPCO — Boring stuff (20%)**
Peak "boring but essential." KEPCO is the national electricity monopoly — it keeps the lights on for the whole country. Utilities are the definition of steady, unsexy, defensive holdings. Bonus: it trades in the US as ADR `KEP`.

**3. Korea Zinc — Commodity / crops (20%)**
The teaching moment. Korea is resource-poor — it grows and mines almost nothing — so its "commodity champion" isn't a farm or an oil field, it's a *smelter*. Korea Zinc is the largest zinc and lead smelter on the planet. Authentic, global, and it makes the point that Korea's edge is processing, not raw resources.

**4. HYBE — Consumer / culture (20%)**
The single purest way to buy "Korean culture." HYBE is the label behind BTS and the engine of the global K-pop wave. This is the fashion/culture slot doing exactly what it should — capturing the soft-power export that made Korea famous worldwide.

**5. KB Financial Group — Banking (10%)**
Korea's largest financial group. Every country portfolio needs a banking anchor, and KB is the blue-chip choice — big, stable, dividend-paying, and available as US ADR `KB`.

**6. Hanwha Aerospace — Wildcard / growth (10%)**
The momentum bet. Korea has quietly become a global arms exporter, and Hanwha Aerospace (howitzers, jet engines, defense systems) has been one of the biggest beneficiaries. High-growth, high-story — exactly what a wildcard slot is for.
